# Agency and source marks

Agency and source marks belong to their owners. Veer is a prototype and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

The marks below are used as-is: trimmed and resized only, never redrawn or recoloured. Most are not freely licensed
(their own file pages say so); they stand for the agency or the data source they name, beside that name, and for
nothing else. Where an agency has no mark on file (Ahmedabad Fire Services, ERSS 112, Jan Rakshak volunteers), Veer
shows its own generic badge instead.

In Veer the PNG marks are further downscaled to at most 128 px tall and 600 px wide (they are drawn at 20 to 56 px):
resize only, the sizes quoted per mark below are the curated files' sizes before that step.
